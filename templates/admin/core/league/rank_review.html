How the flags work

A player needs at least {{ thresholds.min_games }} games before anything is flagged.

  • Consider moving up — rank below {{ thresholds.max_rank }}, winning {{ thresholds.up_pct }} or more of games, and at least 50% of games when giving weight.
  • Consider moving down — rank above {{ thresholds.min_rank }}, winning {{ thresholds.down_pct }} or less of games, and 50% or less even when getting weight.

Once ball counts per game are recorded, this page will add spot-adjusted margins and a "would they still have won at the next rank" test, which are much sharper signals than game counts.
